Responsible Gaming Policy

At All Rummy King, we firmly advocate that online entertainment, card games, and mobile skill gaming should remain controlled, balanced, and recreational. We promote safe digital practices across all applications indexed on our platform.

Mandatory 18+ Age Restriction & Risk Notice: All real-cash skill-based games listed on this portal are strictly intended for adults aged 18 and older. Playing involves financial risk and may lead to habit formation. Play responsibly within predefined limits.

1. Core Principles of Responsible Play

Real-money online skill games like Rummy, Teen Patti, and casual card contests should never be treated as an investment scheme or a reliable method of making steady income. Keep these fundamental rules in mind:

  • Play Only for Entertainment: Treat participation strictly as a leisure activity with disposable leisure funds.
  • Set Strict Budgets: Determine your loss and deposit thresholds beforehand. Never gamble money needed for rent, bills, education, or family essentials.
  • Never Chase Losses: Attempting to recover previously lost amounts often leads to compounding risks. Stop playing immediately if your budget limit is hit.
  • Manage Playing Time: Establish fixed session limits so gaming never interferes with work, study, or family commitments.

2. Underage Protection & Verification

We do not support or direct minors under 18 to real-money gaming environments. We advise parents and guardians to utilize system-level parental controls to restrict minors from installing third-party APK packages or accessing payment credentials.

3. Restricted Jurisdictions Notice

In adherence to regional state laws, players residing in states with statutory bans—including Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Nagaland, Odisha, Sikkim, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Arunachal Pradesh—must not participate in real-cash competitions.

4. Seeking Assistance

If you or someone in your family experiences symptoms of problem gambling or compulsive play, we urge you to step back and seek professional advice or counseling through independent digital helplines.
